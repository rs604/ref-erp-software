Whether a challan was returnable is not a field in Busy. It was typed into the description in free words — "MATERIAL SEND FOR CUTTING", "SEND FOR REPAIRING", "TOOLS RETURNING BACK AFTER" — differently every time. So a pending-returnables list cannot honestly be built from this data. It is searchable though: type repairing to see every item ever sent out for repair.
Type the size exactly as it appears — 80X40X2.5 and 80X40X3 are different pipes, so a number must match before anything is offered. Spacing and capitals do not matter, and the words can be in any order.

GST
Estimate only, not a bill. Some input credit is not eligible, and reversals, reverse charge and credit notes all change the figure. Your accountant works that out at filing. This screen does not know any of it, so it is never labelled payable.
GST actually paid is not shown yet. It lives in the payment vouchers (Busy type 19), and the parser currently reads only the item lines of bills, challans and orders. Showing what was really paid needs the parser extended to read those vouchers — a decision for Raghbir, since the parser is proven and not to be rewritten casually.
Why a 2018 invoice can never change.
Each sync compares only the years that are still on the office PC. Anything in those years that has gone from Busy is marked deleted here — the row is never removed. Once you take an old year off the PC it is frozen: kept here for good, never compared again, never touched. You keep the current year and the one before it, because late entries get posted into the previous year. So the older years simply stop changing, and nothing is lost.
